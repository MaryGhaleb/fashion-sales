## The 6 most important findings

1. **Category leader — Outerwear.** It brings in **$4,045,992** (31% of all revenue). Accessories is the smallest at $1,306,281.

2. **Channel split — Online wins.** Online drives **$6,966,152** vs $6,254,329 for the other channel. Store has a notably lower return rate (10.0% vs 24.7% Online).

3. **Year-over-year — +6.4%.** Revenue grew from 2024 to 2025. Biggest riser: Dresses; biggest faller: Footwear.

4. **Discounting doesn't clearly lift volume.** Discount-to-units correlation is **+0.51** (near zero) — bigger markdowns are not reliably tied to more units, so they may erode margin.

5. **Ratings reduce returns.** Rating-to-return correlation is **-0.35** — higher-rated products get returned less.

6. **Ad spend is most efficient in Outerwear.** ROAS there is **8.52x** (vs 2.81x for Accessories). Overall ROAS is 5.66x.

## What to do next (suggested actions)

- **Double down on high-ROAS categories** to scale efficiently.
- **Review blanket discounting** — it isn't driving volume, so target it.
- **Investigate Online returns** (higher than Store) to protect margin.
- **Plan inventory around seasonal peaks** visible in the monthly trend.
